How to Play

  • Tap Tung Tung Tung Sahur: Every tap drums up Tangsahurs.
  • Hire Buildings in the Store: Buildings automatically generate Tangsahurs per second (TpS) even while you rest.
  • Buy Upgrades: Boost your click power, double building output, and unlock Grandma Synergies & Milk Kittens.
  • Catch Golden Tangsahurs: Watch the screen for shimmering Golden Tangsahurs that grant Frenzy (×7 TpS), Click Frenzy (×777 click!), Lucky! bursts, or Sahur Storms!
  • Harvest Sahur Lumps: Once your bakery matures, wooden Sahur Lumps sprout over time. Harvest ripe lumps to level up buildings (+1% TpS each!).
  • Ascend via Legacy: When you've baked mountains of Tangsahurs, Ascend into the Astral Plane to claim permanent Heavenly Chips and unlock celestial perks.

The Legend of Tung Tung Tung Sahur

At precisely 3:00 AM, when sleepy neighborhoods drift through pre-dawn quiet, an ancient hollow wooden kentongan awakens with legs, eyes, and an unstoppable rhythm: "Tung! Tung! Tung! Sahur!"

Legend says if someone hears the call three times and fails to wake up for Sahur, the wooden drummer invites them straight into a cosmic bakery of infinite Tangsahurs.
